// how it works
1
Install the add-on
Find "Table to Markdown" in the Google Workspace Marketplace and click Install. Takes under 30 seconds.
2
Select your range
Highlight any cells in your spreadsheet — including the header row — that you want to convert.
3
Open the sidebar
Go to Extensions → Table to Markdown → Convert. The Markdown appears in the sidebar instantly.
4
Copy & paste
Hit the Copy button and paste your perfectly formatted Markdown table wherever you need it.
